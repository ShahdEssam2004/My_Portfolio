Front-End Developer

I'm a Computer Science student at Benha University (2022–2026) , and I've been obsessed with the web since the first time I made a button change color on hover. That tiny moment of control turned into a real passion for UI/UX and front-end engineering.

Along the way I've sharpened my craft through hands-on training at NTI Web Masters GDG IEEE Delta Geno Academy — programs that pushed me to ship real, polished projects instead of just tutorials.

Today I focus on writing clean, accessible, performant interfaces — the kind that load fast, behave the way users expect, and look great on every screen. I care about the small details: the easing of an animation, the rhythm of spacing, the clarity of a label.
